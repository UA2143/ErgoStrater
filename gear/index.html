Before you buy

Follow this upgrade order

01 — Fix your position first

Adjust your chair, desk, monitor and laptop position before buying new accessories.

02 — Solve the biggest problem

If your monitor is too low, fix the screen position. If your chair is uncomfortable, prioritize seating.

03 — Buy only what adds value

Accessories should solve a specific problem, not simply make the desk look more expensive.
